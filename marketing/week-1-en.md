## DM sequence (one link only, no shorteners, one question per message)

**1. Auto-reply to the keyword (EN)**
> Hey! Thanks for the comment 👋 I'm from Mirelli Studio. So I send the right demo: what kind of business do you run, and how do bookings work for you today?

**2. After they answer (you, manually)**
> Got it. Here's the closest demo, with sample data so you can click through it: [link]. If something like this would fit your [garage / clinic / salon], I can show how it would look with your services and prices. No obligation.

**3. If they're interested**
> Great. Two quick questions: how many bookings a week do you handle, and what annoys you most about the current way? I'll come back with a rough scope and a fixed written estimate.

**4. If they go quiet (once, after 3 days, then stop)**
> No worries if now isn't the time. The demo stays online if you want to come back to it.

## Warm outreach (15 min a day, 5–10 businesses)

1. Find local businesses with no online booking: Instagram search (`autoservis Bratislava`, `kosmetika Praha`, `Zahnarzt Wien`, `Autowerkstatt Berlin`) and map pins.
2. For a week: follow, leave 2–3 specific comments (not "nice!"), react to stories.
3. Then one personal DM, with the matching demo, no template feel:
   > Hi [name], saw that bookings at [business] go by phone/DM. We build online booking for [garages/clinics] — here's a demo that matches the bay, the mechanic and the parts in stock: [link]. If you'd like, I can show how it would look with your services. No obligation.
4. No answer = no second message.
