# How we work with you (draft for the founder to approve)

This is a draft one-pager to send to a client after the first conversation. It is NOT on the website. Every line below is a promise: keep only what you are ready to do for every project, change the rest, and delete anything you cannot stand behind. (Buyer-persona feedback: a document beats words, and an invented promise is worse than none.)

---

## The first conversation (name: "First conversation", 30 minutes)

- Not called "free consultation" or "discovery call". Both buyer personas said those words sound like a sales funnel.
- Before the call: three short written questions (how many locations, what hurts most, which systems you use today). It shows you prepare.
- On the call: you ask, you listen. No slides and no demo unless the client asks. Camera on if possible, especially for clients in Kazakhstan, where seeing the person matters. For Europe, camera optional but introduce yourself and say where you work from.
- At the end, an honest answer: we are a fit, or we are not and here is who to ask instead. This is the most expensive gesture a studio can make.
- Within 24 hours: a one-page written summary (what you heard, the proposed next step). The price comes after that, in writing, once the task is clear. Both buyer personas expected at least a rough range or the logic behind the price by then; hiding it completely reads as pricing by the client's face.

## What you can rely on (fill in only what is true)

Already stated on mirelli.eu (safe to repeat):
1. Scope and price agreed in writing before work starts, as a contract or a simple written agreement.
2. A working version shown every week.
3. Hosting in the EU or on the client's own server, no lock-in.
4. You deal with a person, not a ticket system.

Proposed additions (each needs your decision before it goes in any client document):
5. The code lives in the client's repository from day one, with documentation to deploy it. Choose: yes / only after final payment / no.
6. Payment in stages tied to accepted results. Choose the split (for example no more than 20-30% upfront).
7. A named backup person who knows the project (even an external contractor on retainer). Choose: yes (name) / not yet.
8. Support with written response times. Choose the times; do not promise 24/7 as one person.
9. For clinics and any health or personal data: a data processing agreement, the hosting location named, and a written answer on access to EU personal data from outside the EU, checked by a lawyer (a first conversation with a lawyer is recommended before the first clinic project).
10. A pilot on one location or one process before a full rollout.

## What we do not do

Write here what you will say no to (for example: websites only, projects below your minimum budget, work you cannot support). A visible "no" is what makes "selected" believable. Do not publish "selected projects only" or "limited places" until this list and a real limit exist.

---

Notes from the four buyer personas (restaurant group, clinic chain, Kazakh entrepreneur, positioning strategist):
- Closedness reads as emptiness without visible work. Show real work, a name or voice, and at least two references a client can call.
- Premium here means predictability and responsibility, not black-and-gold styling.
- In Kazakhstan the decision is made by recommendation, then WhatsApp, then a meeting. Instagram is a showcase.
