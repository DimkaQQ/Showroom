## DM (tone: calm, brief, courteous)

**1. First reply (keyword PROJECT)**
> Thank you for your interest. To prepare something meaningful, may I ask what you'd like to build, and for what kind of business?

**2. After they answer**
> Thank you. That is clear. A few more questions so we can propose the right approach: what exists today (a website, a spreadsheet, nothing), what you'd like to change first, and your timeframe.

**3. Qualification (privately, not published)**
- Is the scope serious (not "a website for €[low]")?
- Is the budget at least `[MIN]`?
- Is it a business we want to work with?

**4. Fit: next step**
> Thank you. This is the kind of project we take on. I'll prepare a written outline and an estimate, and we can speak this week.

**5. Not a fit (polite no)**
> Thank you for thinking of us. This isn't the right fit for our studio at the moment. We wish you every success with it.

Rules: no bulk messages, no links in the first message, one question at a time, reply within an hour on weekdays. Official automation tools only.
