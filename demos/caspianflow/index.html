Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about CaspianFlow.

What is CaspianFlow and who is it for? ▾
CaspianFlow is a B2B energy and fuel marketplace designed for traders, refineries, distributors, and industrial buyers operating in Kazakhstan, Azerbaijan, Turkmenistan, Uzbekistan, Russia, and neighboring markets. It provides a verified environment to post and discover bulk fuel offers with transparent, auditable pricing.
How are prices determined on the platform? ▾
Benchmark prices in the Price Reference section are aggregated from major regional commodity exchanges, published government tariffs, terminal spot data, and broker feeds. They are updated every 15 minutes during trading hours and are indicative — final transaction prices are agreed P2P between buyer and seller.
What payment methods and currencies are supported? ▾
Transactions are denominated in USD as the regional industry standard, with optional display in EUR, KZT, and AZN. Payment is handled off-platform between parties via SWIFT, escrow, or letters of credit. CaspianFlow does not intermediate funds — it is a discovery and introduction platform with optional broker facilitation.
What KYC requirements apply to sellers? ▾
All sellers must complete KYC Level 2 verification: business registration documents, authorized signatory ID, and proof of fuel origin (supply contract or refinery allocation letter). Verification typically completes in 2–5 business days. Professional plan subscribers receive a fast-track review within 24 hours.
Can I use the platform API to pull live prices? ▾
Yes — the REST and WebSocket price feed API is available to Professional and Enterprise plan subscribers. It provides tick-level price updates, historical OHLCV data, and listing webhooks. API keys are generated from Settings → API Access. Rate limits apply per plan tier.
