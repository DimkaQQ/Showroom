Frequently Asked Questions
How CaspianFlow works for buyers and sellers
What is CaspianFlow? ▼
CaspianFlow is a B2B digital marketplace for petroleum product trading across Central Asia and the Caspian region. It connects verified fuel buyers and sellers in Kazakhstan, Russia, Uzbekistan, Azerbaijan, Turkmenistan, Kyrgyzstan and Tajikistan on a single platform.
Who can use the marketplace? ▼
CaspianFlow is for registered businesses only — fuel distributors, trading companies, industrial end-users and refineries. All accounts are identity-verified before listing access is granted. Private individuals are not eligible.
How are prices on the platform set? ▼
Prices shown on the Marketplace are set by individual suppliers and buyers posting their own offers. The Prices screen shows aggregated reference data compiled from member activity and public trade sources — it is indicative, not a live exchange rate.
What does the Starter plan include? ▼
Starter ($199/mo) unlocks contact details for all marketplace listings, lets you post up to 20 buy or sell offers per month, access direct messaging with counterparties, set price alerts, and view your deal history and analytics dashboard.
Does CaspianFlow execute trades or handle payments? ▼
No. CaspianFlow is an introduction and information platform. All negotiations, contracts, and payments happen directly between buyer and seller. We do not act as a broker, agent, or clearing house for any transaction.
What currencies and incoterms are supported? ▼
All prices on the platform are displayed in USD. Listings can specify any incoterm (DAP, FOB, CIF, EXW, etc.) — the incoterm is shown in the Route / Delivery column for each listing.
How do I post a listing? ▼
After subscribing to Starter or Enterprise, go to My Listings and click "New Listing". Specify deal type (BUY/SELL), product, price, volume, delivery terms, and your contact preferences. Listings go live immediately after a brief automated compliance check.
